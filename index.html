Lights Out: Prison Escape is a top-down stealth prison escape game with a real story. By day, Danny Reyes works, crafts and trades inside Rustgate County Jail. By night, he shorts out the lights and slips past the guards in the dark.

30 nights, 3 escape plans, 30 pieces of evidence and 3 endings. No energy, and it plays offline.
