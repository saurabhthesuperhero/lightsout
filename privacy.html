Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Android game Lights Out: Prison Escape (package com.lightsoutgame.prisonescape, "the game", "we") uses, and why. The short version: the game has no accounts and no analytics of its own. Your progress stays on your device. The only third parties are Google AdMob (ads) and Google Play (purchases and updates).

1. Data we do not collect

  • No account, sign-in, name, email address or phone number.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own (no Firebase, no third-party analytics).
  • We do not run a server that stores data about you.

2. Data stored on your device

Your save game (nights played, stars, items, story choices, evidence found), your settings, your consent choices and "what you own" flags for purchases (for example, that Remove Ads is active) are stored only in the game's private storage on your device. We cannot see them. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them. Android may include this data in your own device backup, under your Google account's backup settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch, and occasional full-screen ads between nights or when you retry a night. There are no banner ads, no ads when you open the game and no ads during a night. To show and measure ads, the AdMob SDK collects and shares with Google:

  • device identifiers: the advertising ID and the app set ID;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • app interactions with ads (views, taps) and diagnostics and crash data from the ads SDK;
  • basic device information (model, OS version, language).

Consent. On first launch, and where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the game shows Google's consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before requesting ads. Personalised ads are only shown if you agree. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

The game asks AdMob for ads suitable for a Teen audience (max ad content rating "T"). Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Buying Remove Ads or the Starter Pack turns off the between-night and retry ads. Rewarded ads stay available only when you choose to watch them.

4. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases (Remove Ads, a Starter Pack, stamp packs and cosmetic outfits) are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google Play tells the game which items you own, and the game stores that on your device. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play terms.

5. Game settings download

The game may download a small settings file (game balance values, such as how often ads may appear) from a web address we control. This request contains no personal data; like any web request, it reveals your IP address to the server that hosts the file, which we do not log or use to identify you. If the download fails, the game uses the settings built into it.

6. Children

The game is rated for teens and is meant for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of Google Play's Families programme. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and data was collected through ads, contact us and we will help you ask Google to delete it.

7. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. Your save data is on your device (delete it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling). For data collected by Google for ads, use your consent choices in the game, your Android ad settings, or Google's tools at myaccount.google.com. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have further rights; contact us and we will help.

8. Security

Data sent by the ads SDK and Google Play is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Purchases are verified by Google Play.

9. Changes

If we add a feature that changes what data is used (for example, analytics or online features), we will update this page and the "Last updated" date before that version is released, and update the Data safety section on Google Play.
